# Glossary (seed — verify precise/technical definitions before citing)

- **Phenotype** — the observable characteristics of an organism resulting
  from genotype × environment (× management) interaction.
- **HTP** — High-Throughput Phenotyping.
- **Genotype-to-phenotype gap** — the bottleneck where genomic data outpaces
  the ability to measure phenotypes at matching scale.
- **Vegetation index** — a value computed from spectral bands (e.g.
  combinations of red/near-infrared) used as a proxy for plant status;
  many named variants exist — verify the exact formula before citing one.
- **Canopy cover / ground cover** — proportion of ground area covered by
  plant canopy as seen from above, usually from RGB or aerial imagery.
- **Biomass proxy** — an imaging-derived measurement (e.g. projected leaf
  area, 3D volume) used to estimate biomass without destructive harvest.
- **Segmentation** — separating plant pixels/points from background in an
  image or point cloud, a prerequisite step for trait extraction.
- **Root system architecture (RSA)** — the spatial configuration of a root
  system; often studied via X-ray CT, rhizotrons, or gel/soil imaging.
- **MIAPPE** — Minimum Information About a Plant Phenotyping Experiment,
  a metadata standard (verify current version before citing).
- **Genotype × Environment (G×E) interaction** — the phenomenon where a
  genotype's phenotype depends on the environment it's grown in, central to
  why phenotyping must often be done across multiple environments.
